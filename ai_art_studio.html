Why this project?
“The goal is not to make AI create my art.
The goal is to see what happens when AI looks at it.”

The artist defines the questions and the vocabulary. Pretrained AI models provide another perspective. The interesting part is the comparison: What did I intend? What did the model see? Where did it disagree?

This demo uses pretrained models that run in the browser through Transformers.js. No OpenAI-style token billing or personal API key is used.
